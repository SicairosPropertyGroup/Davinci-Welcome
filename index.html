What Makes a Great Housemate

1

Treat shared spaces like they're your own

If something's out of place, put it back. If something's dirty, wipe it down. If trash falls, pick it up. Small habits — clearing the disposal, restoring a space the way you found it — mean everyone gets to enjoy the same clean, comfortable home you do.

2

Say it directly, and say it kindly

If something's bothering you, talk to the person first — face to face, not in the group thread. Most things get resolved in one honest conversation. Assume good intent before you assume the worst.

3

Give your roommates room to live

Respect quiet hours, knock before entering shared spaces, and remember that "shared" doesn't mean "available to everyone at once." A little consideration goes a long way toward making the house feel comfortable for everyone.

4

Show up for the community, not just the room

Introduce yourself, stay engaged on BAND, and do your part when it's your turn — a shared chore, a quick hello, a load of laundry moved on time. A house feels like a home when everyone's genuinely part of it.

A Couple More Things Worth Knowing

  • Awareness matters, too. See something out of place? Set it right. Hear a smoke detector battery beeping? Say something instead of walking past it. Small moments of attention keep the whole home running smoothly for everyone.
  • Monthly deep cleaning isn't a reset button. Our professional service handles the deep clean once a month — but wiped counters and cleared dishes in between are on all of us, every day.
  • What's not yours isn't yours to use. Not sure if something's shared or personal? Just ask in BAND before using it.
  • Accidents happen — own them. If you break or damage something, don't hide it. Let us know and we'll figure out together what needs replacing or repairing.
